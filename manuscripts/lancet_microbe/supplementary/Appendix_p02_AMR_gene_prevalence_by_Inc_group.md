**Clinical interpretation:** IncN plasmids represent the highest per-plasmid AMR risk (85.3% carriage, 6.23 mean genes), consistent with their established role as efficient multi-drug resistance vehicles in nosocomial settings. IncHI2 plasmids, though less prevalent, carry the second-highest AMR burden (7.42 mean genes) and are associated with large conjugative resistance gene arrays implicated in hospital outbreaks. The combination of high AMR and high virulence carriage on IncFII plasmids (23.2% VIR+) raises concern for co-selection of pathogenicity and resistance under antibiotic pressure.
