## Backbone / accessory partition (IncX1 dataset, n=701)

- Mean backbone length: 37,787 bp (60% of total plasmid)
- Mean accessory length: 25,192 bp (40% of total plasmid)
- Backbone range: 48–82% across individual plasmids

## Mosaicism detection

- **Method:** GC-content variation across 1-kb non-overlapping sliding windows
- **Mosaic candidates:** 302 of 701 IncX1 plasmids (43.1%)
- **Mean GC-SD in mosaics:** 0.047 (range 0.017–0.115)
- **Non-mosaic GC-SD:** 0.021 (range 0.008–0.032)

**Clinical interpretation:** The high proportion of mosaic IncX1 plasmids (43.1%) confirms that these plasmids are active vehicles for acquiring foreign DNA modules — including AMR gene cassettes — from diverse phylogenetic backgrounds. The 40% accessory fraction represents the "cargo space" available for resistance gene acquisition and exchange.
