## Multi-drug resistance co-carriage

- 2+ drug classes: 3,891 plasmids (83.5% of AMR+)
- 3+ drug classes: 3,012 plasmids (64.7% of AMR+)
- 5+ drug classes: 1,847 plasmids (39.7% of AMR+)
- 7+ drug classes: 589 plasmids (12.6% of AMR+)

**Clinical interpretation:** Nearly two-thirds of AMR-positive plasmids carry resistance genes spanning three or more drug classes, confirming that plasmid-mediated multi-drug resistance is the rule rather than the exception. The 39.7% of plasmids carrying resistance to five or more drug classes would confer XDR phenotypes in a single conjugation event. This underscores the importance of tracking plasmid lineages — not individual resistance genes — for surveillance.
