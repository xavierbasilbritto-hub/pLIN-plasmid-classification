# Appendix p 5: Clinically critical resistance determinants — detailed breakdown

## Carbapenemase genes (1,635 detections)

| Variant | Detections | % of AMR+ plasmids | Primary Inc types | Clinical impact |
|---------|------------|-------------------|------------------|----------------|
| blaKPC-2 | 824 | 17.7% | IncN, IncFII | Class A serine carbapenemase; hydrolyses all beta-lactams; inhibited by avibactam (ceftazidime-avibactam active) |
| blaNDM-1 | 228 | 4.9% | IncFII, IncX3 | Metallo-beta-lactamase; hydrolyses all beta-lactams except aztreonam; NOT inhibited by avibactam |
| blaKPC-3 | 193 | 4.1% | IncN, IncFII | KPC variant with enhanced carbapenem hydrolysis; similar therapeutic implications to KPC-2 |
| blaNDM-5 | 89 | 1.9% | IncFII, IncX3 | NDM variant with increased carbapenem hydrolysis; global spread on IncX3 plasmids |
| blaIMP-4 | 66 | 1.4% | IncHI2, IncN | Metallo-beta-lactamase; endemic in Asia-Pacific; similar resistance profile to NDM |
| blaOXA-48-like | 42 | 0.9% | IncFII, IncR | Low-level carbapenem hydrolysis; often combined with ESBLs for clinical resistance |
| blaVIM variants | 38 | 0.8% | IncN, IncI | Metallo-beta-lactamase; endemic in Mediterranean region |

**Therapeutic guidance:** KPC-producing isolates remain susceptible to ceftazidime-avibactam in most cases. NDM/VIM/IMP producers are resistant to all approved beta-lactam/beta-lactamase inhibitor combinations except cefiderocol and aztreonam-avibactam. Co-carriage of KPC + NDM (detected on 12 plasmids in this dataset) would eliminate all beta-lactam options.

## Colistin resistance genes (204 detections)

| Variant | Detections | Primary Inc types | Geographic associations |
|---------|------------|------------------|----------------------|
| mcr-1.1 | 83 | IncI2, IncX4, IncHI2 | Globally disseminated; first detected China 2015 |
| mcr-8.1 | 27 | IncFII | Predominantly Asian; K. pneumoniae associated |
| mcr-8.2 | 18 | IncFII | Variant of mcr-8; similar geographic range |
| mcr-10.1 | 13 | IncFII | Emerging variant; limited epidemiological data |
| mcr-3.5 | 9 | IncFII, IncHI2 | Asian and European isolates |
| Other mcr variants | 53 | Various | Multiple emerging variants under surveillance |

**Clinical significance:** Colistin is frequently the last therapeutic option for infections caused by carbapenem-resistant organisms. Co-carriage of mcr genes with carbapenemase genes on the same plasmid or in the same host creates pan-drug resistant phenotypes with no reliable treatment options. In this dataset, mcr genes were identified on plasmids co-carrying carbapenemase genes in 18 instances — representing the most therapeutically dire scenario.
